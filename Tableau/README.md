# 📊 Employee Attrition & Workforce Analytics — Tableau

## Overview

This Tableau dashboard provides an interactive visualization of **employee attrition and workforce patterns** using a dataset containing **10,000 employee records**.

The dashboard was developed to help identify workforce trends and understand how factors such as department, overtime, job satisfaction, work-life balance, and job level relate to employee attrition.

---

## 🎯 Dashboard Objectives

The Tableau analysis aims to:

* Analyze employee distribution across departments.
* Compare employees who stayed with those who left.
* Examine attrition based on overtime.
* Analyze attrition by job satisfaction.
* Analyze attrition by work-life balance.
* Analyze attrition across different job levels.
* Present workforce insights through interactive visualizations.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Components

The Tableau dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### Employee by Department

Shows the distribution of employees across different departments.

### Employee Attrition — Stayed vs Left

Compares employees who remained with the organization against those who left.

### Employee Count by Overtime

Visualizes employee counts based on overtime status.

### Attrition by Job Satisfaction

Shows how employee attrition varies across different job satisfaction ratings.

### Attrition by Work-Life Balance

Analyzes employee attrition across work-life balance ratings.

### Attrition by Job Level

Shows attrition patterns across different employee job levels.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights

The Tableau dashboard supports the following workforce findings:

* The overall employee attrition rate was **9.03%**.
* Employees working overtime had a higher attrition rate than employees who did not work overtime.
* Job Level 1 recorded the highest attrition rate.
* Lower job satisfaction levels were associated with higher attrition.
* Lower work-life balance ratings showed higher attrition.
* Employees with shorter tenure generally showed higher attrition than long-tenured employees.
