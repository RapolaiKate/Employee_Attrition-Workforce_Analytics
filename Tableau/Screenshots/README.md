# 📊 Tableau Dashboard Screenshots

This folder contains screenshots of the **Employee Attrition & Workforce Analytics** dashboard created in Tableau.

## Dashboard Visualizations

The screenshots showcase:

* Employee distribution by department
* Employees who stayed vs. left
* Employee count by overtime
* Attrition by job satisfaction
* Attrition by work-life balance
* Attrition by job level

## Purpose

These visualizations provide a clear and interactive view of employee attrition patterns and workforce trends identified during the analysis.

The Tableau dashboard is part of the complete **Employee Attrition & Workforce Analytics** project, which also includes Python, SQL, Power BI, and Machine Learning.

**Tableau Desktop**
