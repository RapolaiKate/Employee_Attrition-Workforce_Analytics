## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard visualizations highlight several important patterns:

* Employees working overtime showed higher attrition.
* Lower job satisfaction was associated with higher attrition.
* Level 1 employees showed higher attrition than Level 4 employees.
* Shorter-tenure employees showed higher attrition.
* Employees without recent promotions showed higher attrition.
* Lower income groups showed higher attrition than the highest income group.
